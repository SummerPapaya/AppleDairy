🍎 Embed “An Apple A Day”

Drop the widget into any page on your website.

Option A — one script tag

<div id="apple-a-day"></div>
<script src="HOST/embed.js"
        data-target="#apple-a-day"
        data-mode="calendar"
        data-height="640"></script>

data-mode can be calendar or gallery.

Option B — plain iframe

<iframe src="HOST/?mode=gallery"
        style="width:100%;height:640px;border:0;border-radius:18px"></iframe>
